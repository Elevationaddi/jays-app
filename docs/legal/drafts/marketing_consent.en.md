# Marketing consent policy

> **Draft for legal review.** A UAE lawyer has not reviewed this wording. Items marked [●] are to be confirmed.

## 1. Marketing is optional

You can use JAYS without agreeing to marketing. Agreeing to marketing is separate from accepting the terms and conditions.

## 2. What you can agree to

You choose each channel separately:

- **Email** offers and news from JAYS;
- **Push notifications** with offers and news;
- **SMS** is not used at this stage.

Transactional messages, such as order confirmations, appointment reminders, refunds and security alerts, are not marketing and are sent whatever you choose.

## 3. What we record

For each choice we record what you agreed to or withdrew, the time, where you did it (sign-up, settings or an unsubscribe link), the language and the version of this policy.

## 4. Changing your mind

You can withdraw at any time in Account settings or with the unsubscribe link in any marketing email. Withdrawal takes effect immediately for new messages.

## 5. Sellers

Sellers do not receive your consent to market to you through JAYS unless a future version of this policy says so and you agree.
