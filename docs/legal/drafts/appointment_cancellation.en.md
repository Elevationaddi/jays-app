# Appointment cancellation policy

> **Draft for legal review.** A UAE lawyer has not reviewed this policy against UAE consumer protection law. Items marked [●] are to be confirmed.

## 1. The policy you see is the policy you get

Each service shows its cancellation policy before you pay. That policy is saved with your booking. A business changing its policy later does not change bookings already made.

## 2. Free cancellation

You can cancel for free up to the cut-off the business sets (by default **24 hours** before the start). You get a full refund.

## 3. Late cancellation

After the cut-off and before the start, you get back the share the business sets for late cancellations (from 0% to 100%), shown before you pay. Once the appointment has started you cannot cancel in the app.

## 4. Missed appointments (no-show)

If you do not come, the business may record a no-show from 15 minutes after the start. You get back the no-show share shown before you paid (0% unless the business sets more).

## 5. Rescheduling

You can move a confirmed appointment yourself until the free-cancellation cut-off, to any free time.

## 6. If the business cancels

If the business cancels, you are refunded in full, including any add-ons and travel fee.

## 7. Home visits

The travel fee follows the [home-service policy](/legal/home_services).

## 8. Paying at the venue

For appointments booked by the business to be paid at the venue, no online refund applies; the business settles any amount directly.
