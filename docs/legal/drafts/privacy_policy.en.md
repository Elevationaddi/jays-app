## 2. What we collect

- **Account data:** name, email address, phone number (not verified unless stated), password (stored only as a secure hash), language.

To run your account, process orders and bookings, take payments and refunds, send transactional emails and notifications, provide support, prevent fraud, meet legal and tax obligations, and, only if you agree, send marketing.
