# Authenticity and counterfeit product restrictions

> **Draft for legal review.** A UAE lawyer has not reviewed this policy. Items marked [●] are to be confirmed.

## 1. Only genuine products

Sellers may list only genuine products they are entitled to sell. Counterfeit, replica, "inspired by" products that use another brand's trademarks, and unauthorised copies are prohibited.

## 2. Proof

JAYS may ask a seller at any time for proof of authenticity or authorisation, such as invoices from the brand or an authorised distributor. A seller that cannot provide it may have listings removed.

## 3. Reports

Brand owners and customers can report suspected counterfeits. JAYS reviews each report, may hide the listing while it reviews, and records its decision.

## 4. Consequences

Selling counterfeit goods may lead to removal of listings, withheld payouts for affected orders, refunds to customers, suspension of the seller and reporting to authorities where the law requires.

If you believe you received a counterfeit item, report it through the app. [● Customer remedies to be confirmed.]
