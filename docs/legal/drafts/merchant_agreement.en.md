# Merchant agreement

> **Draft for legal review.** A UAE lawyer has not reviewed this document. It is not final. Items marked [●] are to be confirmed. Commercial terms below reflect the founder's proposed decisions and are not yet contractually approved.

## 1. Parties

This agreement is between [● JAYS legal entity] ("JAYS") and the business that accepts it in the JAYS business portal ("Merchant"). The person accepting confirms they are authorised to bind the Merchant.

## 2. What JAYS provides

JAYS provides an online marketplace where the Merchant can list products and services, receive orders and bookings, and be paid through JAYS's payment provider. JAYS is a disclosed intermediary and is not the merchant of record. The Merchant is the seller.

## 3. Merchant obligations

The Merchant must:

- hold, keep valid and upload every licence and permit its activities require (see the [merchant licensing requirements](/legal/merchant_licensing));
- list only lawful, genuine items and services it is permitted to sell (see the [prohibited products and services list](/legal/prohibited_items) and the [authenticity policy](/legal/authenticity));
- describe items accurately, honour displayed prices, and follow the [promotional pricing policy](/legal/promotional_pricing);
- fulfil orders and perform appointments as promised, and handle returns and cancellations under the policies shown to customers;
- issue tax invoices where it is VAT-registered and give JAYS its correct tax registration number (TRN);
- protect customer personal data and use it only to fulfil the order or booking.

## 4. Fees

- Registration is free and there is no subscription.
- JAYS charges a commission, by default 10% of the eligible price before VAT, on products and services. No commission is charged on VAT, delivery charges or separately itemised home-service travel fees.
- Approved pilot merchants may receive 0% commission for their first 30 days; the default rate then applies automatically. JAYS gives notice before the rate changes.
- Payment processing fees charged by the payment provider are [● to be confirmed: who bears them].
- VAT on commission applies only if and when JAYS is VAT-registered.

## 5. Payments and payouts

Customer payments are collected by JAYS's payment provider for the Merchant. Earnings become eligible for payout [● 7] days after delivery or completion and are paid weekly, less commission, refunds, chargebacks and any amount held under section 7.

## 6. Refunds and disputes

Refunds follow the policies shown to the customer. When the Merchant cancels, the customer is refunded in full. The Merchant bears refunds and chargebacks for its sales; JAYS returns its commission on refunded amounts in proportion.

## 7. Holds, suspension and termination

JAYS may hold payouts, hide listings or suspend the Merchant for suspected fraud, unlawful items, expired licences, repeated complaints or breach of this agreement, and will tell the Merchant why unless the law prevents it. Either party may end this agreement on [● notice period].
