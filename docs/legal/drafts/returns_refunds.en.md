# Product return and refund policy

> **Draft for legal review.** A UAE lawyer has not reviewed this policy against UAE consumer protection law. Items marked [●] are to be confirmed.

## 1. Who handles returns

The seller of the product handles your return. JAYS provides the return request in the app and processes the refund through the payment provider.

## 2. Return window

You may ask to return most products within **14 days of delivery** (proposed). The policy that applied when you ordered is kept with your order.

## 3. Items that cannot be returned

Unless they are faulty or not as described, the following cannot be returned [● list to be confirmed by counsel]:

- products that are unsealed and cannot be resealed for health or hygiene reasons (for example cosmetics, fragrances and personal care items);
- personalised or made-to-order items;
- perishable goods;
- categories the seller or JAYS marks as non-returnable before you buy.

## 4. Faulty, damaged or wrong items

If an item is faulty, damaged on arrival or not what you ordered, tell us through the app [● within X days] with photos. You will not pay to return it.

## 5. How refunds work

- Once the seller accepts the return and receives the item (where it must be sent back), the refund is issued to your original payment method.
- Refunds include VAT in the same proportion as the item.
- Delivery charges are refunded when the whole order is returned because of the seller's error [● confirm].
- When a seller cancels an order, you are refunded in full.

## 6. How long refunds take

The payment provider usually returns funds within [● 5–10] business days; your bank may take longer.

## 7. Disputes

If you disagree with the seller's decision, contact JAYS support. Support is available 9 AM to 9 PM daily and responds within 24 hours.
