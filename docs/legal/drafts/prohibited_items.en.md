# Prohibited products and services

> **Draft for legal review.** This list is not exhaustive and has not been reviewed by a UAE lawyer. Items marked [●] are to be confirmed against federal and emirate rules.

## 1. Principle

Sellers may list only products and services they may lawfully sell in the UAE and in the emirate where they operate. JAYS may remove any listing and suspend any seller that breaks this policy.

## 2. Not allowed on JAYS

- Medical and clinical services and treatments, prescription medicines and medical devices (JAYS does not operate in the medical or clinical category).
- Weapons, ammunition, explosives and their parts.
- Narcotics, controlled substances and related equipment.
- Alcohol and tobacco products [● confirm, including e-cigarettes].
- Counterfeit, replica or unauthorised branded goods (see the [authenticity policy](/legal/authenticity)).
- Stolen goods or goods without lawful title.
- Items or services that require a licence the seller does not hold.
- Adult content and services.
- Gambling products and services.
- Hazardous materials and chemicals not permitted for consumer sale.
- Items that infringe intellectual property.
- Items or content offensive to public morals or religion under UAE law.
- Any item recalled or banned by a UAE authority.

## 3. Restricted categories

Some categories may require additional approval or documents before listing [● to be defined with counsel, e.g. cosmetics registration].

## 4. Reporting

Customers can report a listing from the product or service page. JAYS reviews reports and records its decisions.
