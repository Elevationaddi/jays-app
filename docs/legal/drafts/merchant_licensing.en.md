# Merchant licensing requirements

> **Draft for legal review.** This document does **not** state the licensing rules of any emirate. Which licences and permits apply to each activity in each of the seven emirates must be confirmed by a UAE lawyer; see the legal review checklist. Items marked [●] are to be confirmed.

## 1. General requirement

Every merchant must hold, and keep valid, the trade licence and any activity permits that the competent authority in its emirate (or free zone) requires for what it sells or provides on JAYS.

## 2. What JAYS asks for

During onboarding the merchant uploads:

- its trade licence (with number, issuing authority and expiry date);
- its VAT registration certificate and TRN, if registered;
- for home services, the licence or permit that allows services at customers' homes;
- any additional document JAYS requests for a restricted category.

A JAYS administrator reviews the documents before the business can sell or take bookings. Approval by JAYS is not a legal confirmation that the merchant is properly licensed; the merchant remains responsible.

## 3. Expiry

Merchants must upload a renewed licence before expiry. JAYS may pause listings or home services when a licence expires.

## 4. Changes

Merchants must tell JAYS of any change, suspension or cancellation of a licence.
