# Review moderation policy

> **Draft for legal review.** A UAE lawyer has not reviewed this policy. Items marked [●] are to be confirmed.

## 1. Who can review

Only customers who received a product or attended an appointment can review it, once per purchase or appointment. This means every rating comes from a verified purchase.

## 2. What reviews may contain

Reviews should describe your genuine experience. They must not contain:

- abuse, threats, hate speech or discrimination;
- personal data of others (phone numbers, addresses);
- content that is false and harmful to reputation [● defamation standards to be confirmed];
- advertising, links or spam;
- content offensive to public morals or religion under UAE law.

## 3. Sellers

Sellers may reply once publicly. Sellers may not offer rewards for reviews or pressure customers to change them, and may not review their own items.

## 4. Reporting and decisions

Anyone can report a review. A JAYS moderator may hide a review that breaks this policy and records the reason. Hidden reviews do not count towards ratings. JAYS does not remove reviews just because they are negative.

## 5. Ratings

Ratings shown are averages of published reviews only.
