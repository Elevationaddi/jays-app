# Promotional pricing and genuine previous-price requirements

> **Draft for legal review.** A UAE lawyer has not reviewed this policy against UAE consumer protection and pricing rules. Items marked [●] are to be confirmed.

## 1. Was-prices must be genuine

When a seller shows a previous price ("was" price) next to a lower price, the previous price must be one at which the seller genuinely offered the item for a reasonable period before the promotion [● period to be confirmed, e.g. at least 28 days in the previous 90].

## 2. How JAYS shows discounts

- The was-price is shown crossed out, with the current price.
- The saving percentage is rounded down so it never overstates the discount.
- A was-price must be higher than the current price; raising the price to or above it ends the promotion automatically.

## 3. Seller responsibilities

Sellers must keep records that support a was-price and provide them to JAYS on request. Sellers must not:

- inflate a price shortly before a sale to make a discount look larger;
- show a was-price for an item never sold at that price;
- use countdowns or "limited stock" claims that are untrue.

## 4. Platform promotions

Promotions run by JAYS itself, such as the pilot merchant commission offer, are separate from customer prices and do not change prices shown to customers.

## 5. Enforcement

JAYS may remove a was-price or listing and suspend a seller that breaks this policy.
