# Home-service policy

> **Draft for legal review.** This policy, in particular the travel-fee rule, is **proposed** and must be reviewed for UAE consumer protection compliance before it is final. Items marked [●] are to be confirmed.

## 1. Which businesses can visit you

Only businesses JAYS has approved for home services can offer them. To be approved, the business uploads its licence for home services, which a JAYS administrator reviews. [● Which licence is required in each emirate is being confirmed with counsel; see the legal review checklist.]

## 2. Where they visit

A business visits only the areas it covers. You choose a saved address in a covered area when you book. A minimum service price may apply.

## 3. Travel fee

A business may charge a travel fee for your area. It is shown as a separate line before you pay and on your invoice. JAYS takes no commission on the travel fee.

## 4. Travel time

The professional's travel time before and after your visit is reserved, so another booking cannot overlap with it.

## 5. If you cancel

- **Before the professional sets off:** the travel fee is refunded in full whatever the time of cancellation. The service part follows the [appointment cancellation policy](/legal/appointment_cancellation) shown before you paid.
- **After the professional has set off:** the cancellation policy shown before you paid decides how much of the travel fee is refunded.
- The professional or business records "on the way" and "arrived" in the app. Each step is saved with the time and cannot be changed. "On the way" can be recorded no earlier than 3 hours before the start.

## 6. If the business cancels

You are refunded in full, including the travel fee.
