## Context

ADR-0007 listed fifteen founder decisions, and ADRs 0012–0017 built on documented assumptions where a decision was missing. On 28 November 2026 the CEO answered all fifteen and set out phases 8–12. This ADR records the answers. It says which earlier assumptions they replace, what still needs sign-off, and which phase implements each one.

## Consequences for earlier ADRs

- **ADR-0015 (ledger):** it assumed JAYS is merchant of record. Under D3 the merchant is the seller of record. JAYS collects through Stripe Connect on the merchant's behalf and earns a commission. The double-entry ledger stays. Phase 8.4 relabels accounts and adds the commission, VAT and invoice records needed for a disclosed-agent model.
- **ADR-0013 / ADR-0015 (VAT):** "no VAT on commission" is withdrawn. VAT on commission depends on the platform's registration, and is configurable.
- **ADR-0016 (returns, support, notifications):** the 14-day window and 24 h SLA are confirmed as proposals. Support hours become 9 AM–9 PM, shown to customers. The mock notifier is replaced in phase 9.
- **ADR-0017 (infrastructure):** the AWS target, preferably the UAE region, is set. Phase 10 writes it as code, and nothing is provisioned without approval.

## Guardrails that do not change

- Production still refuses to start with any mock provider, local storage or the mock scanner (ADR-0006).
- No live payments, production tax rules, external deployment, paid infrastructure, approved legal documents or pilot launch without explicit founder approval.
- Nothing invented: no credentials, provider approvals, legal approvals or test results.
